## Candidates rejected during crop review

- Patagonia: [Milan Malfait's portrait](https://unsplash.com/photos/red-glow-illuminates-rugged-mountain-peaks-above-a-serene-lake-KjSemLmT_yY), 3217 × 4826. A wide crop removes the illuminated peaks. Replaced with Marek Piwnicki's landscape sunrise at Torres del Paine.
- Iceland: [Hans Hamann's portrait](https://unsplash.com/photos/waterfalls-during-daytime-tBDe9RY9e_8), 4160 × 6240. A wide crop removes the waterfall context. Replaced with v2osk's landscape of the same Háifoss location.
- Kyoto: [Cosmin Serban's portrait](https://unsplash.com/photos/black-pagoda-temple-during-daytime-jZyQV3I2rT4), 4574 × 6501. Wide cropping cuts off the pagoda. Julen Rey Azcona's and Marek Piwnicki's pagoda alternatives were also portrait compositions. Replaced with Nathalie Lays' landscape of Higashiyama's rooftops and streets in evening light.

Patagonia, Iceland, Cappadocia, Petra, and Samarkand have explicit desktop focal positions in the hero data. Istanbul has a mobile focal position to retain the waterfront mosque. Swiss Alps and Seoul retain their existing center crops.
